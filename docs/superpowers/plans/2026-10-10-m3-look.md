# M3 — the look, the light and the moments (record)

The user, 2026-10-10: "I issue you full creative freedom for these next parts please go wild with it" (after the M2
greybox: "I'd love to see it ooze with style down the road"). M2 merged to master; this work is on branch m3. What was
built, and why, so later passes can build on it rather than re-derive it.

## Art direction: Sunset Print

LA as a 70s screen-printed travel poster that tips into neon noir after dark. At most two type families (Archivo for
signage, Bodoni italic for the sky and neon), as the tenets ask.

- **Sky** (`look/skydome.ts`, keyed by `drive/sky.ts`): horizon-to-zenith gradient printed in 9 bands, a banded glow
  round the sun, the sun itself (HDR, blooms) setting over the sea just before the tunnel (azimuth from the coast road's
  heading), stars after. The fog fades into the horizon colour.
- **World** (`look/toon.ts`): toon shading in three flat tones; LA stucco pastels, golden fields, violet-warm asphalt,
  dark palm silhouettes. Long hard shadows from the low sun (one 2048 shadow map following the car), moonlight after.
- **Print pass** (`look/print.ts`): ink outlines where 1/depth bends (silhouettes and outward corners; flat ground at a
  grazing angle stays clean), halftone dots and a violet shade in the shadows (strong colours — the car, neon — keep
  their hue), then the engine's bloom and paper grain.
- **Night** (`look/night.ts`, one uniform NIGHT): lit windows on the buildings, streetlights passing the car every two
  beats with pools of light, neon and tunnel lettering in HDR, the car's headlights throwing light on the road and its
  triple tail lights glowing.
- **The car**: a '64 Impala convertible (extruded profile, chrome spear, whitewalls), him in a cap at the wheel, her
  hair down.

## The finale

The hill climbs 40 m; the road ends at a cliff just past where the car parks. Below, a city of ~25k lights
(`look/city.ts`: street grids, freeways streaming) switches on in a wave from the overlook outward as the outro starts.
The outro's home framing is low behind the car, the city beyond; its sky lines are shot from the back seat.

## Moments

- The title in the world: the motel's neon sign (SZA / BMF / NO VACANCY) behind the car in the intro.
- "Blowin' money fast": bills pour out of the car (`look/money.ts`) — left on the wind in the tunnel, flung off the
  overlook in the outro.
- "crash" jolts the frame with a flash; the frame punches on each kick while the drums play.
- The back-seat shot (chorus 1, outro): over their shoulders at the sky-writing.
- Boards carry poster art (sunsets, palms, stars, stripes, route shields) until their lyric flips in — never blank, and
  never the song ahead.

## Guard additions

The car is an occluder (`CAR_BOX`): no phrase is ever hidden by it while sung. Road-paint framing follows the grade.

## Bugs found on the way

- The engine runs with autoClear off; the flat background used to clear each frame. The sky dome doesn't: the scene
  clears its target itself.
- `centerAt` returned the carrier's own vector for still carriers; a shot that aimed under the text moved the text.
  It returns a copy.
- Multisampling samples just past a quad's edge, where uv < 0: `pow` of it is NaN, which bloomed into a white bar.
  Glow shaders clamp their uv.

## Open

- Verses: the car is still mostly a passing blur in the across-the-road shots; shot variety there is the main M4 job.
- The back-seat shot can't serve chorus 4 (on the steep turning climb the car really does hide the sky-writing).
- Bills aren't occluders in the guard; they're kept away from the back-seat lens by flinging them forward.
- Preview cost ~98 ms/frame with the GPU shared (greybox ~65): a preview-quality switch (smaller shadow map, 2x MSAA)
  if it gets in the way.

## After the stranger test (2026-10-10)

A subagent reviewed the piece cold (harsh brief). The user: "good review, please implement all changes", keeping their own
emphasis words and the ads (improved), fixing only the worst fragments. Done, phase by phase:

- **Bugs and legibility**: the town's buildings are in the model (blocks.ts) and the guard checks they never hide a word
  or swallow the camera; the camera is never inside the ground, rock, a building or a board (every frame); cuts (on
  half-beats) instead of moves wherever a move would sweep through the town, dip under the ground or change scale
  wildly; unsung words read clearly; no centre line under road paint; road shots hold the car pulling up; bills keep
  off the words and the lens; far carriers put away; sky-writing clears once read; chorus 4's ransom line on gantries
  (the switchback hid the paint).
- **Structure**: the opening cut on its downbeats (neon flickering on, the Impala dropping on its hydraulics, over their
  shoulders, the lot, the pull-out); the end ("crash" whites out to black, the last line over the tail lights, the motel
  again: NO VACANCY loses its NO, fade); the lowrider dances (hops, squats on "dropping", three wheels on "crip
  walkin'").
- **Hooks**: "He's so fly, fly" written whole by a skywriting plane, bigger each chorus; "dropping" drops; "Cruise all
  night" a giant banner; the tunnel's camera travels, its lines sodium then cyan.
- **Craft**: 14 ad products, matched wittily, neighbours never repeating; misregistered plates and paper fibre; the town
  dressed (window glass by day, awnings, rooftops, power lines); the couple's body language (her arm up, her look to
  him, his nod, his hand on the wheel); the song's places as city signs just after their lines.

Bugs found on the way: an escaping slip wrote backspace characters into two regexes (the fly hook never matched);
a dash squashed to zero length had NaN normals (a white dot); the fly line's aim was taken after its time shift.
